\section{Conclusion}

\cite{ko_lstm} did a similar investigation and found that incorperating sentiments from not only news, but forum posts was found to improve the performance of stock price prediction models. It is therefore reccommended that additional sources of data are considered in future work, whilst considering copyright, and terms of use of these sources. More sources would address sentiment sparsity for a single source, and also reduce bias in the training data.

Future studies should increase the scope of this investigation to scrape more websites. More methods of stock price prediction should also be explored such as SVM and RF.
