\section{Machine Learning}

After having sraped the articles for the documents \note{and sentiments?} a machine learning model was developed to predict stock price. Machine learning, and particularly neaural networks, are prevailent in the field of stock price prediction \cite{al_literature_review}. \Cref{fig:nn,fig:rnn} show the structure of a typical feed forward neural network and a recurrent neural network. Recurrent neural networks (RNNS), and others like RNNS, are designed to solve time series problems, and are therefore particularly well suited to this task. RNNs, while powerful, have difficulties with 'memory' due to the number of layers after multiple recursions; so a subset of RNNs, LSTMs allow retaining information for longer periods of time \cite{ko_lstm}.

\cite{ko_lstm} show that an LSTM model, when trained on timeseries price data and sentiment data from news articles, can outperform a model trained on price data alone. Analysis of the literature suggests that SVM models, whilst the most popular \cite{al_literature_review} often underperformed in a financial setting, due to parameter optimisation being required \cite{al_literature_review,zhang_markov_model}
